## 🧠 What Is Content Factor?

Content Factor is a **proof-of-work engine** for builders.  
It captures commits, retros, and technical artifacts, transforms them into structured content, and publishes them with clarity and visibility.

The pipeline flows through five stages:

- 🧱 **Build** — raw commits and technical work  
- 🔍 **Capture** — structured reflections and snapshots  
- 🔧 **Transform** — templates, tokens, and branded formatting  
- 📣 **Publish** — Markdown, dashboards, and social snippets  
- 📊 **Measure** — visible progress and artifact metrics
